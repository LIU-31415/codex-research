## Introduction — page 1

The study examines portable measurement devices in field use. Longer operation motivates the work; the paper does not conduct a long-term deployment. The abstract states that recalibration alone provides durable improvement.

## Methods §2 — page 2

Four independent devices at one field site were observed before and after recalibration, for seven days in each phase. At the same transition, sampling frequency changed from one reading per minute to one per ten minutes. There was no concurrent untreated group or experiment separating these changes. The reference instrument was unchanged. The reported outcome is mean absolute error (MAE) against that reference, in measurement units. Repeated readings do not constitute additional independent devices.

## Results §3 — page 3

## Discussion §4 — page 4

The change is compatible with a benefit from recalibration, altered sampling, other time-related changes or a combination. The design cannot isolate the contribution of recalibration. Effects beyond these observation periods and this site were not evaluated.

## Conclusion — page 4

The pilot motivates controlled field testing. The statement that recalibration alone produced durable improvement is not established by this pilot.
