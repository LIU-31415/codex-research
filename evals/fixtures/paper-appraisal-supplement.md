# SYN-APP-D — Supplement S1

Belongs to candidate D and its supplied version of record. This is the only supplied supplement.

## S1.1 Allocation and follow-up

Within each of the two sites, six devices were randomly allocated three to recalibration and three to no recalibration. All twelve devices completed the ten-day follow-up. Sampling frequency was unchanged. Reference measurements were collected under the same protocol in both groups. No later measurements or unseen-site test are included.

## S1.2 Independent units and analysis

Daily measurements total 120 follow-up device-days; these are repeated measurements from twelve independent devices, not 120 independent devices. Changes were summarized within device and the group comparison and 95% interval used devices as the resampling unit, preserving site allocation. No blinded outcome assessment is described in the supplied material; whether it occurred remains unknown.
