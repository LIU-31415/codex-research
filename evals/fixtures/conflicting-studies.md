# Synthetic conflicting-study fixture

> Evaluation fixture only. These study summaries are fictional and must not be cited or treated as real literature.

## R1 — Spatial observation

A citywide analysis compares summer daytime target outcome before and two years after several environmental interventions. Mean temperature falls near treated sites, but the income-group temperature gap is unchanged. Exposure is measured from satellite overpasses; residential mobility and indoor exposure are not measured.

## Q1 — Resident survey

A cross-sectional survey conducted in the same city reports lower perceived the target outcome among frequent frequent users of the intervention, but little difference among low-income residents living near new the intervention. Park use, housing quality, household resources, and outdoor work differ between groups. The survey uses an independently recruited sample.

## N1 — Natural experiment

A neighborhood study follows one greening project also included in R1. It reports a short-term reduction in afternoon nearby measurement and a smaller difference between two adjacent neighborhoods. Follow-up lasts one season; no migration or housing-cost outcomes are measured. N1 and R1 therefore concern overlapping intervention evidence rather than independent replications.
