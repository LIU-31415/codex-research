# Synthetic conflicting-study fixture

> Evaluation fixture only. These study summaries are fictional and must not be cited or treated as real literature.

## R1 — Spatial observation

A regional analysis compares an outcome before and two years after several environmental interventions. The mean outcome improves near treated sites, but the subgroup gap is unchanged. Exposure is measured from periodic remote observations; residential mobility and indoor conditions are not measured.

## Q1 — Participant survey

A cross-sectional survey in the same region reports a better self-reported outcome among frequent users of the intervention, but little difference among a disadvantaged subgroup. Baseline conditions, household resources, and time spent outdoors differ between groups. The survey uses an independently recruited sample.

## N1 — Natural experiment

A local study follows one intervention also included in R1. It reports a short-term improvement in a nearby measurement and a smaller difference between two adjacent areas. Follow-up lasts one season; migration or cost outcomes are not measured. N1 and R1 therefore concern overlapping intervention evidence rather than independent replications.
