### Methods excerpt

Twelve low-cost sensors were compared with the same reference instrument for 48 hours at fixed temperature and relative humidity. A linear correction was fitted to each sensor's first-day observations and assessed on its second-day observations.

### Results excerpt

## SYN-FIELD — 2023 — One-year indoor monitoring

### Methods excerpt

Eight sensors and a colocated reference instrument were observed for twelve months in one office building. The record identifies the dataset as INDOOR-ONE. Ambient humidity, season, occupancy, maintenance, and elapsed operating time changed together; humidity was not independently manipulated.

### Results excerpt

Errors relative to the reference instrument were larger in humid months. Initial calibration alone did not remove the seasonal pattern. These observations establish a pattern in this building and do not distinguish humidity effects from aging, occupancy, or maintenance changes.

### Limitations excerpt

No second building or independent replication was studied. The report discusses humidity-responsive recalibration as a hypothesis, not a validated intervention.
